# Source Policy

Authority order is official employer career page, verified employer ATS, official corporate/subsidiary page, reputable aggregator, other public source, then unknown. An ATS is official only when registry evidence verifies the tenant. Lower-authority sightings are discovery leads and never overwrite conflicting official facts. Preserve every URL and conflict. Official inactive status overrides an active aggregator; absent official status remains unknown.

An `official_ats` label is not self-authenticating. The source URL hostname must match the employer's `verified_ats_domains` entry in the controlled registry. Unknown tenants receive unknown authority, and domains owned by another registered employer create an ownership conflict requiring manual review.
