# Fit and Scoring

Matches are `proven`, `transferable`, `developable`, `unproven`, `prohibitive`, or `unknown`. Proven and transferable matches require eligible evidence identifiers; other states remain blocked claims. Qualification scoring uses documented weights and gives unknowns no positive credit without treating them as automatic failures. Evidence coverage and confidence remain separate. Strategic value is null without an explicit profile. Opportunity classes preserve gaps and components and never generate resume prose.

Evidence eligibility requires a recognized status, nonempty evidence and source identifiers, an explicit allowed-use list, and a recognized confidentiality class. Résumé alignment additionally requires `resume_alignment` permission and excludes restricted evidence. Public exports redact supported text from private and restricted evidence records.
