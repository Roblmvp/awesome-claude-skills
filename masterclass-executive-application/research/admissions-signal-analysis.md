# Admissions Signal Analysis

Status: COMPLETE — 2026-07-28. Derived from `program-fit-brief.md`. Everything here except quoted official language is inference; the review is a fit screen, not academic admissions.

## Signals the prompt explicitly tests (from its own text)

| Prompt element | What the reader looks for | Weight in scoring matrix |
|---|---|---|
| "Beyond your immediate responsibilities" | Unmandated initiative — nobody asked | Went beyond — 15% |
| "Deeply understand a work process, system, or algorithm" | A real, nameable system; depth, not observation | Clear system — 15%; Systems thinking — 10% |
| "What were you curious about" | An explicit, specific curiosity object | Depth of curiosity — 15% |
| "How did that curiosity influence your motivation" | A visible before/after change in drive | Prompt alignment — 20% (shared) |
| "Why did this experience matter to you" | Personal stake, reflective maturity | Personal meaning — 10% |

## Signals the program values implicitly

- **"Drive to grow"** — their only officially stated selection criterion; the essay must embody it, not claim it.
- **Self-propelled learning** — predicts completion of a self-paced program.
- **Builder orientation** — program promise is "build real tools to solve real problems"; candidates who already do this fit the design.
- **Operator context** — personalization is role-based; a working operator with immediate application surface is the design-target student.
- **Distinct peer-community vantage** — memorable industry perspective beats generic corporate framing.

## Anti-signals (fit killers)

- AI-generated-sounding prose — the reviewers run an AI-native program; assume elevated sensitivity.
- Achievement summary without curiosity arc; resume language.
- "Passionate about AI" without evidence of investigation or building.
- Multiple scattered stories; long background; program flattery.
- Unsupported metrics or inflated claims.

## Calibration notes

- The essay is officially "brief" — concise and plainspoken wins; target well under 400 words.
- Honest limitation ("I didn't know how to advance it") is an asset in a fit review: it is the most credible possible evidence of drive to grow.
- Deadline pressure: Cohort 2 early applications due Aug 4, 2026; decisions in waves, seats limited — earlier is materially better. Final deadline Sep 24, 2026.
