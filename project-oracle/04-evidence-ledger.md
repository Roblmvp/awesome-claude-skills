## Integrity annex (Gate 2: data-quality warnings, conflicts, revisions, unsupported-claim register)

### Data conflicts (unresolved; both readings disclosed wherever used)
1. **H1 2026 total new-vehicle sales y/y:** JDP/GlobalData +1.2% vs Cox "tracking −3.6%" vs S&P −6.7% (Jan–Apr). Retail-vs-total definitions and the 2025 tariff pull-forward base explain part. Treatment: retail −4.1% (well-sourced) used for retail claims; total H1 direction stated as uncertain.
2. **Q4 2025 GDP:** 0.5% vs 1.4% across sources — vintage/revision conflict. Not load-bearing for the forecast; flagged only.
3. **Average new payment:** Edmunds $777 (Q2, financed deals) vs JDP $813 (June, retail transactions) vs Cox $763 (VAI typical payment). Different methodologies; each cited with its owner. Engine cross-check: computed $770 from Edmunds inputs — coherent.
4. **Auto APR:** Experian 6.39% (Q1 contract, prime-weighted) vs Edmunds 7.0% (Q2 financed deals) vs Cox 9.58% (all-credit volume-weighted market rate). All three used, always labeled.
5. **Offset program rate:** 3.75% of MSRP through 2030 vs one report of a 2.5% step-down from May 2026. Unresolved; brief uses "3.75% (possibly stepped down to 2.5%)."
6. **NY Fed auto delinquency "5.6%" vs "2.97%":** 5.6% = share of balances 90+ delinquent (stock); 2.97% = annualized transition into 90+ (flow). Both true; the brief never mixes them.
7. **Lease penetration:** Experian 24.1% (share of new financed) vs Edmunds ~20% (share of transactions). Different bases; both reported.
