# ORACLE Deliverable 3 — Scenario Matrix

**Base date:** 2026-07-28 · Probabilities are post-red-team (Gate 4), sum to 100%. The **supply-shock overlay** (~25%) is not a sixth scenario: it is a production/trade disruption that can land on top of any scenario and inverts the gross-profit logic (volume down, GPU up). Raise/lower conditions govern the monthly re-weighting.

## Supply-shock overlay · **~25% chance of landing on whichever scenario is running**

**Sources:** Section 338 Canada 50% (effective Aug 19) biting as written; USMCA rupture; rare-earth/Nexperia truce lapse (~Nov 1); Hormuz re-closure; a CDK-style dealer-tech outage (operational variant). **Mechanics:** 2021 on a smaller scale — constrained supply against solvent (if narrow) demand → days' supply crashes on affected brands → pricing power returns → volume down, GPU **up**. **Why it's an overlay, not a scenario:** the five scenarios span demand states; this is a supply state, and it flips the P&L sign of whatever demand scenario it lands on (red-team structural finding, adopted). **Dealer play:** it is the one state where offense means inventory — forward-buy parts, pre-order exposed models, hold gross without apology, and do not let 2022's lesson (over-ordering into the unwind) repeat on the way out. **Tells:** brand allocation cuts, OEM force-majeure language, parts backorder spikes, transport dislocation.

## Probability governance

- Re-weight monthly (first week, post-jobs-report) and immediately on any Block-1 trigger.
- Every re-weight must state: what evidence moved, which raise/lower condition fired, and the delta. No silent drift.
- The set must always sum to 100%; the overlay is tracked separately as a conditional.
- Version control: this is v1.0 (2026-07-28). Pre-red-team draft (A30/B27/C23/D15/E5) is preserved in the methodology file's Gate-4 record for auditability.
